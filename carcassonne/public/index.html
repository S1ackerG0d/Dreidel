Carcassonne

The original 72-tile base game, farmers included.

How to play
  • On your turn, rotate (R) and place the drawn tile on a highlighted cell.
  • Optionally place a meeple on a road, city, cloister or field of that tile.
  • Completed roads score 1/tile; cities 2/tile (+2 per pennant); cloisters 9. Meeples return.
  • Farmers lie in fields and stay until the end: 3 points per completed city their field touches.
  • At game end, incomplete features score reduced points.
